Bird Watching at kite Hill

Kite Hill is one of the best places nearby to see birds of prey.Those notes will get you started.

Getting started

You need patience, a pen and binoculars.

  1. Find a quite location facing the hill.
  2. Wait ten minutes without moving.
  3. Write down what you see, with the time.
